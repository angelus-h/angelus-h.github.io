# Photoshop Blackbelt

A rigorous, technically accurate training manual for experienced photographers who want to develop expert-level Adobe Photoshop skills for photographic post-processing.

## Philosophy

This is not a beginner Photoshop tutorial or a collection of tricks.

The goal is to teach you to **understand images**, **understand Photoshop's underlying concepts**, **choose appropriate tools deliberately**, and **perform precise, controlled, preferably non-destructive photographic edits**.

Every technique is taught concept-first: you learn *what happens to the image data* and *why the technique works* before you learn *how to operate it*. Exercises require you to diagnose the image before choosing the tool.

## Prerequisites

You already understand:

- Photography, exposure, lenses, RAW files, composition
- Basic digital image processing concepts
- Photoshop's basic interface (menus, panels, tool palette)

You do not yet have deep understanding of Photoshop's internal concepts, and that is what this manual teaches.

## How to Use This Manual

**Read sequentially for the first pass.** Part I and II are foundational — every subsequent chapter builds on the concepts established there.

**Use as a reference later.** Each chapter is self-contained enough to revisit independently once you understand the foundations.

**Do the exercises.** The exercises are designed to test understanding, not procedure. They require you to diagnose the problem before choosing the tool.

**Challenge levels:**

- **Exercise** — Apply the technique with guidance
- **Advanced Exercise** — Combine techniques to solve a realistic problem
- **Blackbelt Challenge** — Diagnose, choose, and execute without guidance

## Conventions Used in This Manual

**FACT** — What the software or underlying technology actually does. Verifiable in Adobe documentation.

**INTERPRETATION** — How photographers commonly understand or use a feature. Based on professional practice, not official specification.

**RECOMMENDATION** — A practical workflow recommendation from the author. One valid approach among several.

**VERSION NOTE** — Feature behavior that depends on the Photoshop version. Verify in your current version.

**UNCERTAINTY** — Information that could not be confidently verified. Check current Adobe documentation.

---

## Version Notes

This manual was written with reference to Adobe Photoshop 2024–2025 and Adobe Camera Raw. Adobe updates Photoshop frequently, and feature behavior may change between versions.

Where a feature is version-dependent, this is explicitly noted. Where exact current behavior is uncertain, the text says so rather than guessing.

When in doubt, verify against the current Adobe documentation.
