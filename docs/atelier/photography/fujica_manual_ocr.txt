@ MAIN FEATURE

The FUJICA GL690 Professional and FUJICA GM670 Professional are
improved versions of the earlier and popular FUJICA G6Q0BL. They
offer additional handling ease and greater dependability. The 2% x 2%
in. (6 x 7em) format camera (GM670)} joins the 2% x 3% in, (6 x 9em)
format camera (GL690) to offer more effective sports, press, aerial as
well as general photography. These cameras are equipped with the
world’s first electron beam coated lens (EBC FUJINON AE
3.5/100mm) which incorporates all the mechanisms for fully automatic
exposure in the lens itself. Tre precision aperture-preferred fully
automatic exposure system of these cameras lets the photographer
concentrate on taking his pictures without being distracted by the fuss
and bother of exposure setting.

Their main features are as follows.

1. Double-image focusing Coupled range-viewfinder for quick and
smooth focusing, Automatic compensation for parallax and chang-
ing field-of-view.

2. Two-stroke film advance lever advances the film and cocks the
shutter.

3. Takes both 120 and 220 roll film.

4. Frame size: GL690: 2% x 3% in. (6 x 9em)

GM670: 2% x 2% in. (6 x 7em)

5. Perfectly color balanced FUJINON interchangeable lenses from
50mm super wideangle to 180mm telephoto.

6. Between-the-lens shutter synchronizes with electronic flash and
regular flash bulbsat all shutter speeds (Excepting the EBC FUJINON
AE 3.5/100mm lens which is provided with X contact only.)

< How to Use the Depth of Field Scale >

There is a zone of sharpness before and behind the point on which a
lens is sharply focused. It is known as the lens depth of field and its
depth differs with the lens focal length, aperture and shooting
distance

The white aperture numbers engraved on the lens barrel on both
sides of the Lens Setting Mark (long red tine with a ball in the
middle) is the depth of field scale. This is the way to use it

If you are using a 100mm normal lens with the distance set at 15 ft.
(5m) and the aperture at 1/8, the depth of field extends from
14.7 ft. (4.5m) from the camera to 21.6 ft. (6.58m). (See depth of
field tables)

through the viewfinder, focus the lens
and press the Shutter Release Button
gently down.

Always clean the camera before and

should be wiped with silicon cloth,

© Do not put finger marks on the lens and
viewfinder glass. In case you do, wipe

© Keep the camera away from dust and

@ CHECK THE CAMERA
BEFORE SHOOTING

The FUJICA GL690 and GM670 are
designed for professional use. Always
check the camera before use to prevent
costly mistakes.

1, Film Advance Lever

Check the fever to make sure it is working
smoothly. There is a limit to the mecha
nical durability of the film winding unit.
Always ask for an overhaul about once
each year.

2, Rangefinder
Look through the viewfinder and focus
the lens on infinity and on a close-range
subject with the double-image focusing
spot and check the distance scale on the
lens barrel to make sure they agree. All the
better if you can open the camera back
and check the focused image at the film
plane with a sheet of ground glass.
3. Shutter
Set the R-S Button at S, open the camera
back and check the different shutter
speeds with the shutter release button
The quantity of light reaching your eye
and the sound of the shutter will give you
a close indication of the shutter’s condi-
tion. There is also a limit to the mecha-
nical durability the shutter. A mechanical
shutter needs overhauling about once a
year and an electronic shutter about once
every two years.

4, Flash Synchronization
Mount the flash, open the camera back,
open the aperture fully, set the shutter
speed for the flash you are using and
release the shutter to make sure it
synchronizes with the flash
