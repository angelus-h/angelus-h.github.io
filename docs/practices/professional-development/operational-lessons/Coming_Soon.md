## Development Principles

All content in this section follows these principles:

1. **Observable Facts Only** - No speculation without explicit labeling
2. **Anonymized** - Companies, individuals, projects fully anonymized
3. **No Diagnoses** - Focus on behavior patterns, not psychological labels
4. **Operational Focus** - Extractable, actionable lessons
5. **Validated** - Cross-referenced with research where possible
